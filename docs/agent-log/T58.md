## 2026-11-21 · T58 Home page v2 (the approved mockup), stats that respect data gaps · implementer (opus-5.5)
- Did:
  - **Layout.** `app/views/markets.py` is the P-022 Home: top bar (brand, search, one status dot), headline strip, a watchlist rail grouped by asset class (full names, short names, 30-day sparkline, σ tags), the chart card (header with name, price, change pill and a provenance line; range pills, Candles/Line, a `⋯` popover with compare and CSV; the TradingView chart; one row of 1D–1Y changes; a footer line), a right rail (verdict card, risk and range with a labelled 52-week bar, links to the lineage panel and Macro), tabs (All values, Lineage, Checks, Corrections) and a footer (mode, withheld, glyph legend).
  - **Chart card as a fragment.** `_hero` is an `st.fragment` built from `chart.tv_series` / `tv_compare_series` / `tvchart.chart`, so `chart.py` (T57B's file) is unchanged; `chart.price_panel` is not used because its controls sit above the chart, not in the header.
  - **Gap-aware stats** in `tearsheet.py`: `gaps()` (steps of more than 5 business days), horizons refuse a base close more than 5 calendar days (10 for weekly series) older than the horizon's start, vol/sigma/drawdown skip moves across a gap and the card says so, `date_span()` names the year across a year.
  - **Caches** in `instrument.py` and `tearsheet.py` are keyed on `data.data_version()` with the 15-minute TTL.
  - **Carry-overs:** `tvchart.chart` arms the fallback only on an error report not seen before and pops `_fail_key` when the chart draws; the attribution link has no inline colour (the sanitiser stripped it) and inherits the meta colour; the health badge's tooltip shows on hover of its container.
- Decisions:
  - Selection, range and style are mirrored into plain session-state keys (`db-keep-*`) because Streamlit drops a widget's state on a page that does not draw it, and the browser's page change drops `?id=`/`?r=`.
  - The watchlist rail scrolls inside a fixed-height container (730 px, 440 px on a phone): about 50 instruments would otherwise make Home 3,900 px tall.
  - The 30-day small multiples are not moved to Macro (T59 owns the pages): every instrument's 30-day trend is its watchlist sparkline, as in the mockup. The yield curve is on Macro already ("Treasury curve · change by tenor"); Home links to it.
  - Home's CSS for the new blocks lives in `panels.HOME_CSS` (the theme extension was one rule only).
- Gotchas:
  - `page()` in the Home tests now skips `<style>` blocks: the stylesheet names old classes (`.db-move`) that tests assert are gone from the markup.
  - The shared-connection cleanup fixture moved into `conftest.py` (autouse), because the Home tests open enough stores to exhaust native threads in one process.
  - Local port binding and `file://` are blocked for the screenshot browser; the mockup was captured with headless Chrome, outside the sandbox.
