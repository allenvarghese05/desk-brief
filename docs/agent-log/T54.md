## 2026-11-19 · T54 Dashboard views for the new data · implementer (opus-5.5)
- Did:
  - New **Macro** page (`app/pages/2_Macro.py`), linked in the top-bar navigation after
    Markets (`panels.PAGES`). Panels, all in the T36 dark-terminal style:
    - **VIX term structure:** VIX9D, VIX, VIX3M and VIX6M now, about 1 week and 1 month earlier
      (inline SVG: gridlines, lines and points only). A table gives each tenor's level and its
      change in points against 1W and 1M. The 3M − 30D spread is a figure, and the shape is
      stated in words: contango, backwardation or flat, an inverted front end, and a change of
      shape since a month ago.
    - **Treasury curve change:** the curve now, 1M and 1Y earlier, and a table of each tenor's
      yield with its change in bp against 1W, 1M and 1Y. One sentence says whether the curve
      bull- or bear-steepened or flattened over the month.
    - **2s10s:** the latest spread, its change against 1M and 1Y, inverted or positive in
      words, and a daily history line with a zero rule.
    - **Gold against the 10Y real yield:** gold futures (GLD when no futures history is
      stored) and DFII10 on their own scales, with the real yield drawn inverted, as the
      caption says. Figures: both levels, their 1M changes and the 90-day correlation of daily
      changes, plus whether gold is moving opposite to real yields as usual.
    - **Correlation:** a 90-day heatmap of daily changes over SPY, QQQ, gold, 10Y, 10Y real,
      the broad dollar, VIX, WTI and HY OAS. Prices and indices use log returns and yields
      and spreads use bp moves. Every cell is a `value_cell`, and a pair needs 20 common
      changes.
    - **CFTC positioning:** gold, ES and ZN. Each shows the net speculative position, the
      week-on-week change from the report's own `change_text` (e.g. "+12,000 contracts"),
      open interest, the position Tuesday, and a weekly bar history around zero.
  - `app/components/macro.py` (new) holds all the view logic as pure functions over bars.
  - `data.build_series` and `data.load_series`: bars for several instruments through
    `build_bars`, on the shared connection, cached 60 s per mode. They add no new connection
    per click.
  - `app/theme.py`: Macro panel CSS and a dotted curve line for "1Y/1M ago".
  - Tests: `tests/unit/app/test_macro.py` (18 tests, 20 cases) and `tests/unit/app/macro_synth.py`
    (seeded synthetic rows).
- Provenance:
  - Every figure goes through `value_cell` or `change_cell`. A test strips every
    provenance cell, SVG and style attribute from the page and finds no decimal or signed
    number left.
  - The two inline SVGs carry only tenor labels, and a test pins that.
  - Altair charts hide their value axis: every figure is in the strip under the chart.
    Tooltips name tier and source, as T36's price chart does.
  - Every chart has a caption with sources, tier glyphs and the date span.
- Hosted mode: the new reader goes through `build_bars`, which excludes hidden tiers in SQL.
  Tests on the same store:
  - `build_series` with local and hosted exclusions;
  - the page in local mode shows tier-1 SPY in the heatmap;
  - hosted, unset and unknown modes show no `alpaca` and no live glyph.
- Short history: a line needs 20 daily points or 4 weekly reports. With fewer, the panel
  says how many are stored, over which dates, that the line needs more, and that the two-year
  history load fills it. Comparisons before the first stored day say "needs 1W/1M/1Y". A
  comparison point never sits more than 7 days before its target.
- Went wrong:
  - A series with a single stored day (SPY's one tier-3 close in hosted mode) made
    `correlation` compare an empty integer index with a timestamp, and the page crashed. It
    now returns no correlation, and a unit test covers it.
  - The first layout printed the full provenance line in each strip and heatmap cell. On the
    desktop screenshot it overflowed. Strips now use the compact tag (glyph and date
    visible; field and source on hover and for screen readers, as Home's watchlist does).
    Heatmap cells show only the end date, with the span in the hover text.
  - The preview config at the `dashboard-synthetic` path was a T36 copy without the new
    instruments. I rewrote it from the current `config.yaml`, pointed it at a new synthetic
    store (`world54`), and kept the old file as `config.yaml.bak-t36`. Both are outside the
    repo.
- Decisions:
  - One new page rather than more panels on Home: Home is the per-instrument workspace, and
    these are cross-instrument views. Names link to Markets (`./?id=`), so selection stays
    URL-driven.
  - `build_bars` prefers the official tier for a day. A curve tenor held by both FRED and
    Treasury takes FRED (alphabetical, the existing rule), and the caption and each cell name
    the source.
  - CFTC changes come from the report model (T45's `change_text`), so the dashboard, report
    and Excel say the same thing.
- Size: about 1,050 lines added in `app/` and about 450 in tests, over the ~400-line guideline.
  It is not split because the six views share one data path and one page.
