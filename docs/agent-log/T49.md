- Decisions:
  1. **Legacy variant.** It is the only one covering gold and the two financial futures with one
     trader split. Disaggregated has commodities only and TFF financials only. Non-commercial is
     the conventional "speculative" category behind the T45 label.
  2. Futures only, not futures and options combined: it is the headline series, with no delta
     conversion.
  3. **`as_of` is the position Tuesday, not the release**, per the `1w` rule in
     `store/backend.py`.
  4. `net_position` is marked `is_derived`, because CFTC publishes long and short, not the
     difference.
  5. Freshness on `SIFMA_US`. XNYS lacks Veterans Day, which delayed the 13 Nov 2026 release
     to 16 Nov. Monday holidays and Good Friday are over-counted, which means a later `warn`
     and never a false `fail` (known limit in `cftc.md`).
